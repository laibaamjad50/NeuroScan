# EDA Figures — What Each One Tells Us

All 16 figures are generated by `Brain_Tumor_MRI_Phase1.ipynb` (saved at 150 dpi via the
`save_fig()` helper in Section 1) and are also displayed inline in the notebook next to the
code and the markdown that interprets them. The notebook section that produces each figure is
given below, so you can jump straight to the surrounding discussion.

## Data cleaning (Section 4)

| # | Figure | Section | What it tells us |

| 02 | `02_format_consistency.png` | 4.4 | Counts of image format, colour mode and file extension. All files are JPEG, but colour modes are mixed (L, RGB, RGBA, P) — so a greyscale conversion is required before anything else. |

| # | Figure | Section | What it tells us |

| 08 | `08_intensity_violins.png` | 5.4 | Violin plots of mean, std, entropy, edge density, black fraction and skew by class. No Tumor is visibly brighter and more detailed than the tumour classes, so even simple statistics carry class signal (partly a scanner artefact, not biology). |
| 09 | `09_mean_intensity_kde.png` | 5.4 | Density of mean brightness per class. No Tumor peaks around 62 versus about 35 for Glioma, i.e. the distributions are shifted, not just differently shaped. |

## Preprocessing & scaling (Section 7)

| # | Figure | Section | What it tells us |
|---|--------|---------|------------------|
| 16 | `16_scaling_before_after.png` | 7 | One training image and its histogram at three stages: resized (0–255), scaled (÷255 → 0–1), and standardised with the training mean 0.1749 and std 0.1953. The shape of the intensity distribution is preserved while the range becomes small and centred near zero — what a neural network needs for stable training. |

## Main takeaways across the figures

1. **Duplicates and leakage were real** (01) — 438 files, 6.08%, were removed, including 161 training images that also appeared in the test set.
2. **Formats were inconsistent** (02) — mixed colour modes and 446 distinct resolutions, resolved by padding to a square and resizing to 224×224 greyscale.
3. **Outliers are genuine scans** (03, 04) — flagged but deliberately kept.
4. **Classes are close to balanced** (05) — mild class weights are enough.
5. **Resolution is a shortcut risk** (07, 10, 11) — Cramér's V = 0.52 between resolution and class; this is the single most important thing to control for in Phase 2 (hence the uniform resize and the planned Grad-CAM check).
6. **Raw pixels are not linearly separable** (14) — a deep model is justified.
